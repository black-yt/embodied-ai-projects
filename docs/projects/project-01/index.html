Engineering insights

Screen out long pauses

Measure the range of motion in each joint and hand channel over a sliding window. One round of dataset preparation excluded demonstrations with more than two seconds of sustained inactivity. When trimming frames within a usable demonstration, preserve meaningful holds, such as a pause during a press.

Slice and balance skills

Split demonstrations into complete single actions. Keep 2–6 frames of context on each side within verified boundaries, and balance first, middle and final repetitions 1:1:1. Split training and validation by the original demonstration so neighboring clips stay together.

Smooth with minimum jerk

Penalize the third finite difference of the predicted end-effector path, a discrete measure of how quickly acceleration changes. Anchor the measured start and planned endpoint, limit deviations at intermediate points, and preserve the finger and gripper commands.

Predict ahead to reduce waiting

While the current chunk runs, the future dynamics module (FDM) uses those actions to forecast visual features beyond the latest observation. The policy uses that forecast to prepare the next chunk ahead of the handoff. Measured frames correct the history used by subsequent predictions.

Connect end-effector and joint control

An EE output specifies a target tool pose. A joint output specifies target joint angles. Inverse kinematics (IK) converts a tool pose to joint angles, and forward kinematics (FK) converts joint angles back to a tool pose. With a consistent coordinate frame and IK branch, both can represent the same trajectory.
